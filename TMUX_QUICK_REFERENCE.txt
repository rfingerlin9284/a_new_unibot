NAVIGATE PANES:
  Ctrl+B then → to go right to narration
  Ctrl+B then ↓ to go down to status
  Ctrl+B then ← to go back to engine

MAXIMIZE A PANE:
  Ctrl+B then z
  (Press again to return)

SWITCH BETWEEN PANES:
  Ctrl+B then arrow keys
  OR
  Ctrl+B then o (cycles through)

WHEN PANE FREEZES:
  Ctrl+C (interrupt)
  Then Ctrl+B then x (kill pane)

EXIT TMUX (KEEP RUNNING):
  Ctrl+B then d (detaches)

RE-ENTER TMUX:
  tmux attach-session -t rick

CREATE NEW WINDOW:
  Ctrl+B then c

KILL EVERYTHING:
  tmux kill-session -t rick
